# Company Policy Document

## Attendance Policy
Employees must log in by 9:00 AM. Late arrivals beyond 15 minutes may be marked as half-day.

## Leave Policy
All leaves must be approved by the reporting manager. Emergency leave can be applied on the same day.

## Code of Conduct
Employees must maintain professionalism and respect towards colleagues at all times.

## Data Security
Company data must not be shared externally without authorization. Use only company-approved tools.

## Remote Work Policy
Employees working remotely must be available during core hours (10 AM to 4 PM).
