# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Work Hours
Q: What are the working hours?
A: Employees are expected to work from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

## Leave Policy
Q: How do I apply for leave?
A: Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 days in advance via the HR portal.

## Remote Work
Q: Is remote work allowed?
A: Yes, remote work is allowed with prior manager approval.

## Salary
Q: When is salary credited?
A: Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month.

## Support
Q: Who do I contact for technical issues?
A: Contact the IT support team at it-support@company.com.
